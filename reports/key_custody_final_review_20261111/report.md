## Verdict
**The migration plan is sound in structure, but not ready to execute.** Three findings must be resolved first; K-1 is blocking.

| ID | Severity | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| **K-1** | **High (blocking)** | Attribution config v3 accepts the **relay record key pinned as the attribution-result key** (verified). If that misconfiguration were made, whoever holds the relay key could sign attribution results directly and bypass correlation. Key pins must be distinct per role |
| **K-3** | **High** | The backup `cowrie/backups/pre_isolation_20261110/var/lib/cowrie/` holds plaintext copies of the **live Cowrie SSH host private keys** (ECDSA/Ed25519/RSA; fingerprints match the live keys). Their inherited ACL is *BUILTIN\Users: Read* and *Authenticated Users: Modify*. They are git-ignored. They are not attribution keys, but they are the honeypot's SSH identity |
| **K-2** | Low | A corrupt attribution-result public key raises `ValueError` instead of `AssessmentConfigError`. It still fails closed |
| S-1 | Medium (process) | The scratch directory holds 6 files (5 unique attack inputs from Task 26C) that exist nowhere else and cannot be regenerated byte-for-byte |
